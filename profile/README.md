
# Suncrest Labs

We build financial infrastructure for markets that have been underserved by legacy systems — starting with Africa.

Our work sits at the intersection of decentralized finance and real-world usability. We care less about crypto-native complexity and more about whether someone in Lagos or Nairobi can actually use what we ship.

---

## Products

**[Nester](https://github.com/Suncrest-Labs/nester)** — A DeFi savings and liquidity protocol designed for emerging markets. Nester lets users earn yield on stablecoins and access automated off-ramp infrastructure through a network of liquidity nodes.

**Terra** — A Bitcoin-native social protocol built on the Stacks blockchain. Terra gives creators wallet-based identity, AI-powered agents, and a reputation layer built on real on-chain activity.

---

## Philosophy

Good financial infrastructure should be invisible. It should work in the background, move money reliably, and get out of the way. That is what we are building.
